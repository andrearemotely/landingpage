How it works

Three phases, from clarity to a system your business can actually run on.

01

Clarity session

A focused working session to map how your business actually operates today: every step, every tool, every gap. You walk away with a documented picture of where things stand and what needs to change.

02

System design

Based on the clarity session, I design the system: what goes where, how work flows, who owns what, how clients get onboarded. The output is a clear blueprint, ready to be built in ClickUp or Notion by a specialist.

03

Ongoing support

Available when needed

A light monthly check in to catch what's working, what's breaking, and what needs adjusting as your business grows.
