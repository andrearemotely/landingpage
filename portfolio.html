Case study

100+ requests, each with a turnaround within 24 hours

Situation

A small agency managed a client's website under a year long agreement. The client sent feedback and update requests throughout the year, from copy changes to small fixes.

What I did

Set up one process for every request: logged in one place, assigned an owner, and delivered back to the client within 24 hours.

Result

100+ requests handled over the course of the year, each turned around within 24 hours.

Takeaway

Fast turnaround comes from a process that runs the same way every time, not from working harder when things pile up.
